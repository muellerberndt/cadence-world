The rules of the next world

Per tick, per cell: with probability 0.08 × light × fertility a unit of soil becomes food; with 0.01 food becomes soil; with 0.05 a unit of soil steps to a neighbour. Food is drawn as height, unripe food in blue-green, rock as grey blocks.

A creature's reading is a set of field groups: the food, the unripe food, the creatures, the rock, the kind of ground and the kin in its window; its energy, the light, what it last did, whether it is dark, what it carries, and what it heard. Each cortex of its brain reads the groups its mask names through a fixed sparse expansion developed from its genome, and holds one record per active cell and action of the mass that followed. The brain acts on the sum of what its cortices read, or, with a horizon above zero, on a search through the readings its moves would produce; after the outcome every cortex writes the error into exactly the cells its reading touched. A split mutates the genome: the genes, each cortex's mask, size and wiring, and cortices are added and removed.

Each tick a creature loses a unit to the soil under it with probability 0.08 + 0.0004 per unit read + 0.01 per 256 cells + 0.002 per active cell + a tenth of a read per imagined reading + 0.05 if it moved + 0.02 if it spoke + 0.10 if it bit. It splits at the threshold its genome names. At 0 it dies and its records leave the known world.
